# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML / CSS / SCSS / JS. No React, Next.js, Tailwind, or shadcn CLI. Motion via vendored GSAP, ScrollTrigger, Lenis, and SplitType. Content lives in local JS objects. Hosting is static files.

## Users

Primary: technical recruiters, AI/software companies, collaborators, and founders who need to decide in seconds whether Daniel Beni is a credible builder.

Secondary: students, the African tech community, and people arriving from GitHub.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that is itself a designed product. It must prove that Daniel builds intelligent software, ships real products, and creates technical communities — not that he can fill a template.

Success: a first-time visitor knows who he is, sees three real projects, understands the community work, and can contact him without hunting.

## Positioning

Daniel Beni — AI Engineer · Software Builder · Community Builder, based in Yaoundé, Cameroon.

Hero identity is builder + AI + community. Telecommunications engineering student (level 3, SUP'PTIC) appears only in About.

Neighboring generic developer portfolios cannot truthfully claim this mix: published work (CARBURFLOW, Syntra, Mabbtek) plus community building (SUP'PTIC Computer Club, Claude Cameroon).

## Operating Context

Single-page static site. Visitors arrive from GitHub, applications, or a shared link. They scan on laptop or phone, then email or open GitHub. No login, no CMS, no backend in V1.

## Product Principles

1. The site is the first case study: craft must demonstrate the claim.
2. Show evidence, not adjectives. Unverified numbers stay out.
3. Motion explains hierarchy or relationships; otherwise it is removed.
4. Community is a human section, not a product card.
5. Keep the architecture static and extensible — no backend until a real need appears.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, semantic headings, meaningful alt text, and a complete experience when motion is reduced. Animation must never be the only way to understand content.
